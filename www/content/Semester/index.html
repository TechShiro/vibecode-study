How this is set up

  • Each subject is its own teaching workspace — lessons/, reference/, assets/, plus MISSION.md and RESOURCES.md.
  • Lessons are HTML. They open in any browser with no internet needed, and they print cleanly for revision — recall-card answers force open on paper.
  • Quizzes are one-shot. Answering locks the question so the answer cannot be fished for, and options are padded to near-equal length so length is not a tell.
  • Everything is exam-shaped, not mastery-shaped, per your brief. Where a topic could go deeper, the lesson says so and stops.
