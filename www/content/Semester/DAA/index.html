Design and Analysis of Algorithms

COCSC303 · 3L–0T–2P. All five units are built — 28 lessons, five printable exam sheets, and every algorithm, DP table, graph trace, and reduction proof worked in full. Every lesson names the exact concepts, algorithms, and numericals for its topic.

Unit I in one line: how to measure an algorithm — the RAM model, asymptotic notation, recurrences and the Master theorem — ending with a tour of the four design paradigms. Unit II in one line: the data structures and sorting algorithms themselves, from BSTs through the three balanced trees and hashing to the sorting bound and string matching.

Short on time? Lesson 5 first — Master theorem problems are the cheapest marks in this paper and two or three appear every year. Then 13 (sorting and quick sort's derivation), 8 (AVL rotations), 3 (code-fragment complexity), 11 (hashing), 15 (the KMP table).
