Principles of Compiler Construction

CO CSC 502 · 3L–0T–2P. All five units are built — 27 lessons, five printable exam sheets, and every conversion, table, and data-flow trace worked in full. Every lesson names the exact concepts for its topic, with exam-targeted summaries.

Unit I in one line: what a compiler is — the translation pipeline, the six phases, the tools that build them, and how the first compiler ever got compiled. Unit II in one line: the first phase in full detail — from a regular expression describing a token to a minimal DFA that recognises it, which is exactly what Lex generates.

Short on time? Lesson 2 first — the phases with the worked statement is the most-asked question in this entire paper. Then 8 and 9 (the four conversions, which are mechanical marks), 4 (bootstrapping T-diagrams), 10 (Lex), 6 (buffering with sentinels).
