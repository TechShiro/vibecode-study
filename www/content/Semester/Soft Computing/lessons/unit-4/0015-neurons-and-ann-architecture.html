Biological neuron

  • Dendrites: receive input signals from other neurons.
  • Soma (cell body): processes inputs.
  • Axon: transmits output to other neurons.
  • Synapse: connection point between neurons (variable strength = weight).

Mathematical model (McCulloch-Pitts)

Artificial neuron Net input: net = Σ wi xi + b (weighted sum + bias)
Output: y = f(net) where f is the activation function.

Retrieval practice

1. The output of an artificial neuron is:

Weighted sum captures input importance, bias shifts the threshold, activation introduces non-linearity.

2. The sigmoid function outputs in the range:

σ(x) = 1/(1+e^-x) approaches 0 for large negative x and 1 for large positive x, but never reaches either.

3. A multi-layer perceptron (MLP) has:

Hidden layers enable learning non-linear decision boundaries. Single-layer can only learn linearly separable functions.

Recall without looking

Mathematical model of a neuron.

net = Σw_i·x_i + b. y = f(net). f = activation function (step, sigmoid, tanh, ReLU).

Three ANN architectures.

Single-layer (perceptron), multi-layer (MLP, back-prop), recurrent (feedback, Hopfield).
