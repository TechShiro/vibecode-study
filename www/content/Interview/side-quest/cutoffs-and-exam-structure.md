# SSC CGL & CPO (SI) — Structure, Marks, Cutoffs (General/UR)

Ballpark cutoffs from recent cycles (as of mid-2026). Vacancies + paper
difficulty move these every year — before applying (2028), pull that year's
notification + previous cutoff PDF from https://ssc.gov.in.

---

## SSC CGL (post-2022 pattern)

**Stages: Tier 1 → Tier 2 → done. No interview. No physical for most posts.**

### Tier 1 — screening only (marks NOT in final merit)
| Section | Qs | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| Reasoning | 25 | 50 |
| General Awareness | 25 | 50 |
| Quant | 25 | 50 |
| English | 25 | 50 |
| **Total** | **100** | **200** — 60 min, −0.50/wrong |

UR cutoff to reach Tier 2: **~150–160 / 200**

### Tier 2 Paper 1 — the ONLY thing in final merit
| Section | Qs | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| Maths | 30 | 90 |
| Reasoning | 30 | 90 |
| English | 45 | 135 |
| General Awareness | 25 | 75 |
| **Counted** | **130** | **390** — 3/Q, −1/wrong |
| Computer module | 20 | qualifying only |
| DEST (data entry) | — | qualifying only |

- Paper 2 (Statistics) → JSO post only; Paper 3 (Finance & Eco) → AAO only
- Physical: none for most posts; Excise Inspector/Preventive Officer/Examiner/
  SI-CBI have lenient qualifying standards (~157.5+ cm male, token walk/cycle test)

### Final cutoffs by post (UR, /390)
| Post | ~Cutoff |
|---|---|
| AAO | 320–330 |
| ASO MEA / top ministries | 315–325 |
| Income Tax Inspector | 310–320 |
| ASO CSS, GST/Excise Inspector | 300–315 |
| SI CBI / NIA | 300–310 |
| Auditor / Accountant | 285–300 |
| Tax Assistant, UDC/SSA | 270–290 |

Rule of thumb: **~80% in Tier-2 Paper 1 = Income Tax Inspector (UR)**.

## SSC CPO — SI Delhi Police / CAPF

**Stages: Paper 1 → PET/PST → Paper 2 → Medical. No interview.**

### Paper 1
| Section | Qs | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| Reasoning | 50 | 50 |
| GK & Awareness | 50 | 50 |
| Quant | 50 | 50 |
| English | 50 | 50 |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** — 2 hr, −0.25/wrong |

UR cutoff to reach PET: **~145–160 / 200**

### PET/PST — qualifying only
- **Male:** height 170 cm; chest 80 cm (+5 expansion); 100 m in 16 s; 1.6 km in
  6.5 min; long jump 3.65 m; high jump 1.2 m; shot put 16 lb → 4.5 m (3 chances each)
- **Female:** height 157 cm; 100 m in 18 s; 800 m in 4 min; long jump 2.7 m; high jump 0.9 m
- Delhi Police SI males: valid **LMV driving licence** required

### Paper 2 — ENGLISH ONLY
English Language & Comprehension: **200 Qs, 200 marks**, 2 hr, −0.25/wrong.
No other subject. (So English = 250 of the 400 total merit marks.)

### Medical (DME) — qualifying
6/6 & 6/9 **uncorrected** eyesight (LASIK rules: check notification annexure —
pre-op power limits matter for −6), no colour blindness, standard fitness.

### Final merit = Paper 1 + Paper 2 (/400), UR ballpark
| Post | ~Cutoff |
|---|---|
| SI Delhi Police (male) | 290–310 |
| SI Delhi Police (female) | 295–315 |
| SI CAPF (BSF/CRPF/CISF/ITBP/SSB) | 275–300 |

---

## One-line takeaways
- CGL merit = Tier-2 Paper 1 only (390); English + GA are 210 of it.
- CPO merit = P1 + P2 (400); **English alone is 250** — it decides selection.
- Both: no interview. Quant/reasoning are Krish's free marks; English + GK are
  where ranks are made.
