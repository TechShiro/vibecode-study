# Side Quest — SSC/Govt Exam Prep (backup plan)

Target exams (final year, 2028 cycle): **SSC CGL** (primary — Income Tax/GST
Inspector, ASO), **SSC CPO** (Delhi Police SI), banking PO as extras. One prep
covers all — see syllabus overlap notes in the conversation log.

**Prime directive: this NEVER eats SWE prep time.** Budget: ~30 min/day + one
weekend session. If placements heat up, GK daily habit survives, everything else
pauses.

---

## The schedule (start now, runs on autopilot)

### Daily (~20–30 min — memorization needs spaced repetition, not marathons)
- **15–20 min static GK** — one NCERT chapter section OR one Parmar SSC video
  (rotate: Polity → History → Geography → Science/Bio → repeat)
- **5–10 min English vocab** — 5 new words + idioms (any daily-vocab list/app);
  revise yesterday's 5

### Weekly (weekend, ~1.5 hr total)
- **45 min English grammar** — one topic (error spotting rules, tenses,
  prepositions...) + 20 practice Qs
- **30 min quant speed drill** — one topic's worth of SSC PYQs against a timer
  (you know the maths; SSC is a speed game)
- **15 min reasoning** — one PYQ set (you barely need this; keep the muscle warm)
- Once a month: **one full Tier-1 mock** to benchmark (60 min)

### NOT now (final-year only)
- Current affairs (only last ~12 months before the exam matter)
- Paper-2-level English depth, mock grinding, PET fitness prep
