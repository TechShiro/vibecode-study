Classic Concurrency & Synchronization Problems

The famous benchmark problems that test every synchronization architecture.

1 · The Producer-Consumer Problem (Bounded Buffer)

Producer produces items into buffer; Consumer removes them. Solved using 3 semaphores: mutex = 1, empty = N, full = 0.

2 · The Readers-Writers Problem

Multiple readers can read concurrently. A writer requires exclusive access. Solutions prioritize readers (may starve writers) or prioritize writers (prevents stale writes).

3 · The Dining Philosophers Problem

5 philosophers sit around a table with 5 chopsticks. Each needs 2 chopsticks to eat. If all pick up their left chopstick simultaneously, everyone waits forever — Deadlock!

Solutions 1. Allow at most 4 philosophers at the table. 2. Asymmetric rule: Odd philosophers pick left chopstick first; even philosophers pick right chopstick first.
