RAID Storage Architectures

Combining multiple physical disk drives for performance, redundancy, or both.

↑ Back to Course IndexModule D — Storage, File Systems & I/O

1 · The One Idea

RAID (Redundant Array of Independent Disks) organizes multiple physical hard drives into a single logical volume. It balances three pillars: Speed (Striping), Redundancy (Mirroring), and Cost Efficiency (Parity XOR).

2 · The RAID Levels Comparison
