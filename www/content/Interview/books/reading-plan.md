# Reading Plan — Indian History & Hindu Texts

**Goal:** learn my own history and religion from real, authentic books — not reels,
not TV shows, not exam material. This is *not* SSC prep. This is for me.

**Rule:** 20 pages a day, or 30 minutes. Whichever comes first. Physical books.
Phone in another room.

---

## The two kinds of "authentic"

| | Traditional / devotional | Critical / scholarly |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Gita Press, Ramakrishna Math, ISKCON | Penguin (Debroy), Oxford (Olivelle, Jamison) |
| From | within the tradition, with commentary | oldest reconstructed manuscripts, footnotes |
| Gives you | what it *means* to believers | what the text *says* |
| Watch for | each school's interpretive lens | dryness, no spiritual guidance |

Both are legit. Ideal: scholarly for the text, traditional alongside for the meaning.

⚠️ **"Bhagavad Gita As It Is"** (Prabhupada/ISKCON) is the most distributed Gita on
earth and will be pushed at me everywhere. It is a *Gaudiya Vaishnava commentary* with
interpretation baked into the verse translations — not a neutral text. Read it if
curious, but don't mistake it for the plain Gita.

⚠️ **Don't start with the Vedas.** Oldest ≠ first. They're archaic ritual hymns with no
narrative. Everyone starts there, bounces off, quits. Year two.

⚠️ **Not authentic sources** (fine as fiction, not as the thing itself): Amish Tripathi,
Devdutt Pattanaik, Anand Neelakantan. These are retellings/interpretations.

      **₹283**, hardcover, **980 pages**, ISBN `978-1602930001`. Verified in stock 2026-07-14.

      ✅ **It is a SINGLE VOLUME.** No parts, no set.
      ⚠️ Abridged/pocket editions strip the word-for-word Sanskrit — which is the only

      ⚠️ **Srimad-Bhagavatam** is the ~30-volume ISKCON set — that's the Bhagavata Purana
         (Stage 4 below), NOT the Gita. Don't get pulled in by accident.

**Why two Gitas:** there is no lens-free Gita. Easwaran softens the theistic/devotional
Krishna into universal meditation-philosophy (a quiet, unannounced lens). Prabhupada
loads Gaudiya Vaishnava theology into the verse translations themselves (a loud,
declared lens). Read Easwaran; check verses against Prabhupada's word-for-word Sanskrit.
Two lenses beat one — and the Sanskrit beats both.

Worked example, **18.66** (*sarva-dharmān parityajya... mā śucaḥ*):
- Prabhupada: "Abandon all varieties of religion and just surrender unto Me. I shall
  deliver you from all sinful reactions. Do not **fear**."
- Easwaran: "Abandon all supports and look to me for protection. I shall purify you
  from the sins of the past; do not **grieve**."
- *sarva-dharmān* → "varieties of religion" vs "supports" — **both stretching**; *dharma*
  is famously untranslatable.
- *sarva-pāpebhyaḥ* → "sinful reactions" loads ISKCON's karma vocabulary into a noun
  that just says *papa*, sins.
- *mā śucaḥ* → **Prabhupada is simply wrong here**: *śuc* = to grieve, not fear.
- [ ] **The Upanishads — Patrick Olivelle** (Oxford World's Classics)
      *Gentler alt:* Eknath Easwaran.
      Where the actual philosophy lives — Atman, Brahman. Everything downstream is
      built on this.

## Indian history track (run in parallel, one at a time)

- [ ] **John Keay — *India: A History*** — best readable single volume. Start here.
- [ ] **Upinder Singh — *A History of Ancient and Early Medieval India*** — the serious one.
- [ ] **Romila Thapar — *Early India: From the Origins to AD 1300*** — academic standard.
- [ ] **William Dalrymple — *The Anarchy*** — East India Company. Gripping.
- [ ] **William Dalrymple — *The Golden Road*** — India's ancient global influence.
- [ ] **Abraham Eraly — *The First Spring*** (Gupta era) / ***Emperors of the Peacock Throne*** (Mughals)
- [ ] **Ramachandra Guha — *India After Gandhi*** — 1947 onward.

### On bias — read across, deliberately
Indian history is ideologically contested and pretending otherwise is its own bias.
Thapar / Dalrymple / Guha sit in the mainstream-academic camp. **Sanjeev Sanyal**
(*Land of the Seven Rivers*, *The Ocean of Churn*) writes readable history from a
Hindu-nationalist-adjacent view. Read both camps. Becoming someone else's echo is
exactly what I'm trying to escape by getting off reels.
